# References and Attributions

This document lists the primary scientific literature, technical resources, and media assets used in the **RC Audio Lab** project and its accompanying semester research report.

---

## Academic and Educational Sources

1. **Kulhánek, P. / MIT & Aldebaran Group for Astrophysics**  
   *Elektřina a magnetizmus V: Kapacita a dielektrika*.  
   Czech translation: Jan Pacák / Petr Kulhánek. Chapters 5.1, 5.2, and 5.4.  
   Available from: [Aldebaran Kurz 05](https://www.aldebaran.cz/elmg/kurz_05_diel.pdf)

2. **Pacák, J. / MIT & Aldebaran Group for Astrophysics**  
   *Elektřina a magnetizmus VII: Stejnosměrné obvody*.  
   Czech translation. Chapters 7.4 and 7.6 (DC RC transient response and energy dissipation).  
   Available from: [Aldebaran Kurz 07](https://www.aldebaran.cz/elmg/kurz_07_curd.pdf)

3. **Bales, James A.**  
   *Lecture 4: RC Circuits*. Practical Electronics (EC.S06), Fall 2004.  
   Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT OpenCourseWare).  
   Available from: [MIT OCW EC.S06 Lecture 4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/ec-s06-practical-electronics-fall-2004/5d70e7c37b7dc19e1e947e7a7b1d4ac6_MITEC_S06F04_lec04.pdf)

4. **Pacák, J. / MIT & Aldebaran Group for Astrophysics**  
   *Elektřina a magnetizmus XII: Střídavé obvody*.  
   Chapters 12.2–12.3 (AC steady state, impedance dividers, and resonance).  
   Available from: [Aldebaran Kurz 12](https://www.aldebaran.cz/elmg/kurz_12_cura.pdf)

5. **Massachusetts Institute of Technology**  
   *6.117 Lecture 3: Digital circuits, power supplies and regulation*. IAP 2020.  
   Available from: [MIT 6.117 Lecture 3](https://web.mit.edu/6.117/www/lec3.pdf)

## Technical and Audio Engineering Sources

6. **Texas Instruments**  
   *TI Precision Labs – RC circuits: Transient and AC Transfer Functions*.  
   Video tutorial series (December 31, 2020).  
   Available from: [Texas Instruments Precision Labs](https://www.ti.com/video/6119035031001)

7. **Analog Devices University Program**  
   *Chapter 7: Diode Application Topics – Peak Detectors*.  
   Available from: [Analog Devices Wiki Chapter 7](https://wiki.analog.com/university/courses/electronics/text/chapter-7)

8. **Analog Devices Technical Articles**  
   *An Overview of Automatic Level Control (ALC)*. December 22, 2005.  
   Available from: [Analog Devices ALC Overview](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/technical-articles/an-overview-of-automatic-level-control.html)

9. **Fender Musical Instruments Corporation**  
   *Capacitor Ceramic Disc .05µF @ 100V 20% (12)*. Specifications and historical pickup tone network applications.  
   Available from: [Fender Official Store](https://www.fender.com/products/capacitor-ceramic-disc-05uf-100v-20-percent-12)

10. **Seymour Duncan Research & Articles**  
    *250k vs 500k Pots: Going Deeper into the Subject*. Analysis of pickup source inductance, cable capacitance, and tone circuit loading.  
    Available from: [Seymour Duncan Blog](https://www.seymourduncan.com/blog/tips-and-tricks/250k-pots-versus-500k-pots-going-deeper-into-the-subject)
