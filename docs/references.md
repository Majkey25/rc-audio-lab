## Audio

`public/audio/guitar-f2.flac` is the unchanged **F2_s1_01.flac** recording from [FreePats Electric Guitar FSBS clean](https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-clean), version 2026-08-07. The library is published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); a copy is included at `public/audio/CC0.txt`.

The browser makes an eight-note riff by transposing the recorded F2 to E2 and other intervals. A second version moves it down another octave. Short gain ramps separate notes. These are arrangements of a recorded guitar sample, not separate performances recorded for this project. The source recording was already processed through an amplifier/effects chain, as stated in the FreePats README.

## Claim map

- Capacitor energy and RC transients: MIT/Aldebaran chapters 5 and 7.
- Impedance, filter response and resonance: MIT chapter 12 and Practical Electronics lecture 4.
- Actual tone capacitor use: Fender component documentation; pickup loading: Seymour Duncan.
- Envelope detection and timing: Analog Devices university material and ALC application note.
- Browser processing: W3C Web Audio; this repository documents the coefficient derivation.
- AI disclosure: UTB library declaration and citation guidance. Tool/model names in the author declaration describe the reported workflow, not sources for physical facts.
